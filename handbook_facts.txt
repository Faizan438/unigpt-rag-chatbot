International Students Handbook - Admission Criteria
Admission criteria for international students at Superior University: minimum 60% marks in previous academic result; high school certificate (translated into English); IBCC equivalence certificate; valid passport.

International Students Handbook - Intake and Academic Year
Fall intake: applications from April to June. Fall classes start in September.
Spring intake: applications from December to February. Spring classes start in March.

International Students Handbook - Visa Process
The visa process has 4 stages.
1. Admission process: student application, picture, previous academic record.
2. HEC-NOC application: HEC information letter, HEC cover letter, HEC confirmation letter, scanned passport.
3. Embassy NOC: university acceptance letter, HEC NOC, scanned passport, bonafide letter for the visa.
4. Pakistani visa application: scanned photograph, acceptance letter, NOC from HEC, NOC from embassy, NOC from the home country, bonafide letter for visa.

International Students Handbook - Essentials to bring to Lahore
Essential items: passport, study visa, additional proof of identity (driving license or identity card), scanned copies of passport/visa/important documents, proof of residential address in home country, original qualification certificates used for visa and admission application, offer letter provided by Superior University, acceptance letter.
Packing tips: pack light, you can get almost everything in Lahore. Carry valuables in hand luggage. Never put cash or valuables in check-in luggage.

International Students Handbook - Rules after arrival
Fee submission is in 2 installments per semester. 80% attendance is compulsory in each semester. A student is dropped from the course if attendance falls below 75%. Mid-term exams have 30% weightage. Attending all social events organized by the university is mandatory. Fine is charged as per university policy on late fee submission.
Accommodation: Superior University Hostel and Guest House, room types single, double, quad.
Language courses: fall intake December, spring intake April, duration 3 months, 2 lectures weekly.
On-campus internship opportunities: Marketing Department, Communication Department, Global Engagement Office.

International Students Handbook - Semester Exchange Program Eligibility
Semester Exchange Program eligibility: students from 2nd semester till 5th semester can apply. Minimum CGPA required is 2.5. Good communication and presentation skills are needed for the interview. The exchange is for one semester, extendable to one year.

International Students Handbook - Global Pathway Split Degree Eligibility
Global Pathway (split degree) eligibility: CGPA 3.0 and proficiency in English. For the Bachelors program, students of 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th semester can apply. Students start in Pakistan and complete the remainder of the degree abroad.

International Students Handbook - Semester Exchange Application Procedure
Semester Exchange application has 12 steps: 1 fill the application form online, 2 pre-interview, 3 final interview, 4 complete registration if selected, 5 submit documents, 6 coordinate with international office for nominations, 7 list mapped courses on the form and submit to host university, 8 wait for acceptance, 9 apply for visa, 10 attend training sessions, 11 go for exchange program, 12 continue studies at Superior after return.
